Where craft meets heritage

The SANSKAAR Atelier

“For every bride and groom, we don't just tailor fabric — we weave a story of culture, pride and timeless grace.”

At SANSKAAR, every garment represents generations of craftsmanship. Our weavers in Banaras and Kanchipuram use pure metallic zari and handloom mulberry silk. Each piece is then brought to our Jaipur atelier, where master embroiderers spend hundreds of hours on zardozi, gota-patti, mirror work and dabka motifs.
