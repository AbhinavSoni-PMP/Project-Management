Our story

संस्कार — values we wear

Sanskaar was born in the lanes of Jaipur, where every wedding is a celebration of family, colour and craft. We work with more than a hundred karigars across Rajasthan, from zardozi artisans in Jaipur to bandhej dyers in Jodhpur and gota-patti makers in Ajmer.

Each outfit carries their hours of handwork, finished with a modern cut so it fits the way you celebrate today.
